## Who buys
- **Brainspotting practitioners**: therapists who have taken Brainspotting training (often right after Phase 1) and need a pointer for sessions.
- **Practitioners upgrading**: they already own a cheap telescoping pointer and want something that feels personal and well made.
- **Gift buyers**: a colleague, consultant or partner buying for someone finishing training.
